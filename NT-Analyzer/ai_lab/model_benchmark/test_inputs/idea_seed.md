# Idea Seed

Create a simple MNQ/MES intraday pullback strategy for AI_SANDBOX research.

Seed concept:

- Trade only in the first part of regular trading hours.
- Use a trend filter and one pullback trigger.
- Prefer simple indicators such as EMA, VWAP proxy, ATR, or opening range.
- Avoid more than 3 indicators.
- Do not hold overnight.
- Use fixed risk controls before any optimization.
