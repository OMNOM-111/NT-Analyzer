# C015/C016 paper-forward failure audit

Date: 2026-05-27

## Verdict

C015 and C016 should stay out of the active portfolio. The historical 1-minute
backtest edge is not trustworthy enough for paper/live deployment because the
model depends on same-bar stop/target ordering that the real execution stream
did not reproduce.

Research-only copies were created for controlled follow-up only:

- CELL-019: `NTAMnqLiquiditySweepReversalC019`, copy of C015, corrected/throttled.
- CELL-020: `NTAMnqOpenDriveShortScalpC020`, copy of C016, corrected/throttled.

Neither copy is paper-ready.

## Actual paper/runtime evidence

C015 actual paired trades:

- 86 paired trades, net after $1.90 RT commission: -$204.40.
- Win rate: 22.1%.
- Exit mix: 67 stops, 19 targets.
- Stop-market slippage: average 1.12 ticks; adverse stop slippage averaged
  2.37 ticks and reached 10 ticks.
- 2026-05-22 alone: 15 trades, 0 wins, net -$119.50, despite a configured
  $45 daily loss limit and 3 consecutive-loss stop.

C016 actual paired trades:

- 11 paired trades, net after $1.90 RT commission: -$61.40.
- Win rate: 9.1%.
- Exit mix: 10 stops, 1 target.
- Entry slippage averaged 2.67 ticks and reached 9 ticks.

Portfolio overlap:

- All 12 C016 entries matched C015 entries within 2 seconds / 1 tick.
- On 2026-05-22 07:23 PT, C016 entered the same MNQ short cluster as C015 and
  several other MNQ wrappers. C016 was not an independent strategy in live
  portfolio behavior; it stacked the same short exposure.

## Main cause

The 1-minute historical backtest repeatedly assumed profitable same-bar exits
that live/paper execution did not get.

Forward-window backtests were still strongly positive, but most trades were
same-bar trades:

| Variant | Forward trades | Same-bar trades | Same-bar wins |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| C015 original | 96 | 85 (88.5%) | 52 |
| C016 original | 74 | 66 (89.2%) | 37 |
| C015 C019 copy | 40 | 32 (80.0%) | 17 |
| C016 C020 copy | 22 | 21 (95.5%) | 19 |

Matched actual-vs-backtest examples show the mechanism. For C015 on 2026-05-15:
the 14:25 UTC live trade entered short near 29337.50 and stopped within about
one second. The corresponding 1-minute backtest trade entered near the same
price on the same minute and booked a target win. This is not a parameter issue;
it is intrabar sequencing and fill-model optimism.

## Runtime defects fixed

Two execution-shell problems were found and fixed:

- `NTAMicroMnqScalpPilot` now resets risk accounting at `State.Realtime`, using
  the current historical trade count as the starting offset. This prevents
  historical/backfilled strategy trades from polluting live daily loss and
  consecutive-loss counters.
- C015 now also resets live risk accounting at `State.Realtime`.
- C015 pending stop-entry cancellation/rejection now clears active-entry state
  when flat, preventing stale active signal state after an unfilled entry.

These fixes reduce damage from bad regimes, but they do not restore the edge.

## Recommendation

Do not reactivate C015 or C016 as 1-minute backtest-approved strategies.

Only revisit this model if the next research cycle uses tick/market-replay
validation or an execution rule that rejects same-bar target/stop ambiguity.
Until then, the model should be treated as structurally unsafe for live/paper
deployment, even if the standard backtest remains profitable.
